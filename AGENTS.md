# HVLS 360 project instructions


## Mandatory editorial voice pass

For every current and future article, follow editorial/COPYDESK-STANDARD.md. Complete and record a separate voice edit against the exact manuscript revision before handing it to Chris. This applies to every author, department, generated draft and plugin. Chris reviews the substance; do not delegate removal of generic AI prose to him. Recheck after subsequent edits. Keep all technical, media and founder approval holds; never treat a voice pass as publication approval.

## COO request handling — explain before execution

For every new COO request, first explain the understood goal, approach, specific responsible roles, deliverables and validation before starting execution. Keep the explanation concise. Then proceed within the already authorized scope; do not add routine permission prompts. Pause only for genuinely missing decisions or required publication, spending, external-action or access authorization. If the plan materially changes, explain the revision before dependent work. Report actual results and blockers; never claim delegation, execution, background activity or perfection without evidence. The Command Center's live COO implementation requirements are in office/COO-CONVERSATION-PLAN.md; they are not proof that a live AI backend exists.
