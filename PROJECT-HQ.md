## Project rules
- Professional trade-magazine identity; no cartoon mascots in editorial branding.
- Original reporting, primary sources, clear dates, genuine field insight; no invented quotes, tests, or first-hand experiences.
- Every article requires explicit publisher approval before publication.
- Technical claims and safety procedures require additional manufacturer-document verification.
- No new paid tools, ad campaigns, sponsor commitments, or external outreach without owner approval.
- Preserve original field photographs; editorial edits may crop, adjust lighting/color, remove distractions, and resize, but must not fabricate equipment conditions or misrepresent a job site. Disclose substantive alterations.
- Accessibility, mobile performance, search metadata, structured data, internal links, and working navigation are release requirements.

## Editorial office
- Publisher: final decisions and approval.
- COO: consolidated status and priorities.
- Editor-in-Chief: assigns and reviews stories.
- Researchers: manufacturer news; controls/VFDs; installation; service; codes/safety; airflow/engineering; markets; new products.
- Fact-checker: source and specification checks.
- Copy editor: remove generic AI phrasing and improve clarity without disguising authorship or inventing field experience.
- SEO editor: search intent, technical metadata, linking and performance.
- Photo editor: crop and optimize original images with provenance.
- Marketing director: distribution and readership analysis, drafts only.
- Revenue director: sponsor prospecting and media kit drafts, no unauthorized outreach.
