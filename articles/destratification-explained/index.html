Destratification Explained: Why Air Movement Matters

HVLS 360 editorial desk · Educational overview · Not manufacturer-approved service guidance

A practical introduction to temperature layering and HVLS fan applications.

Technical procedures, electrical work and safety-critical decisions must follow the current manufacturer documentation and applicable requirements. No manufacturer-specific specifications are asserted here.

What stratification means

Warm air can accumulate near high ceilings while occupied areas remain cooler. HVLS fans are often used to encourage air mixing, but outcomes depend on building geometry, heat sources, operating settings and other ventilation equipment.

Performance is site-specific

Avoid universal claims about energy savings or temperature reductions. Evaluate the facility's measured conditions, fan placement, operating schedule and heating or cooling system before estimating benefits.

Field considerations

Columns, racks, suspended equipment and mounting clearances can affect airflow and installation feasibility. Use manufacturer layout guidance and site measurements rather than assuming nominal fan diameter alone determines coverage.
