About the publication

Editorial standards & technical review

HVLS 360 Editorial Team · Publication policy

HVLS 360 covers the high-volume, low-speed fan and industrial air movement industry. Our editorial process prioritizes source attribution, technical accuracy and practical value to installers, technicians, engineers and facility professionals.

Research and sourcing

News claims must be linked to identifiable sources. Technical specifications should be verified against the applicable manufacturer's documentation and clearly identified by product model and document revision where available. We distinguish independent analysis from manufacturer claims.

Technical safety

Installation and electrical information is not a substitute for the applicable codes, equipment instructions, qualified judgment or site-specific engineering. Drafts involving electrical work, rigging, fire alarm interfaces or safety-critical procedures require additional review.

AI-assisted workflow

Automated tools may help identify topics and prepare drafts. All published articles require editorial approval. We correct errors transparently and do not fabricate quotes, test data or first-hand experience.

Corrections

When a substantive error is identified, the article should be corrected with a dated note explaining the change.
