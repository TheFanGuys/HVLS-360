Guy Wires and Safety Cables on HVLS Fans: What Four Manufacturers Call For

HVLS 360° Editorial Desk · November 24, 2026

Plan on four guy wires and a safety cable for most HVLS fans you hang. CaptiveAire requires guy wires on every mounting configuration. Hunter's DDI ships with the hardware and treats the guy wires as a standard install step. Big Ass Fans and MacroAir tie them to drop length and mount type [1][2][4][5].

They don't agree on angle, hardware or safety cable slack, so pull the manual for the fan you're hanging before you order eye lags and cable.

When the guy wires go in

  • CaptiveAire: required on all HVLS mounting configurations. A fan hanging more than 10 ft below the point where the guy wires attach, or a longer run, needs an additional cable [1].
  • Big Ass Fans Powerfoil X: required when the extension tube is 4 ft or longer [2].
  • MacroAir AVD 370: required on the Universal and UMH mounts, and never used on the Rapid Commercial Mount. A drop longer than 5 ft rules out the Rapid Mount, so you switch to the Universal Mount with guy wires. MacroAir doesn't recommend a Universal Mount drop longer than 10 ft [4].
  • Hunter DDI: the box includes four beam clamps, four turnbuckles and a Gripple kit, and the guy wires are step five of the install [5].

The MacroAir rule bites on change orders. Drop a Rapid Mount fan past 5 ft and you're changing the mount and adding four wires.

Angle and layout

All four use four wires. CaptiveAire and MacroAir space them about 90 degrees apart, so the anchors land at the corners of a cross centered on the mount [1][2][4][5].

The angle off the ceiling varies:

  • MacroAir and Hunter: 45 degrees between the ceiling and the wire [4][5].
  • Big Ass Fans Powerfoil X: 30 to 45 degrees from the roof to the extension tube [2].
  • Big Ass Fans guy wire sheet for the Essence, E Series, Pivot 2.0, Yellow Jacket and Sweat Bee: drawn at 45 degrees, with a call to customer service if the angle is off by more than 15 degrees [3].
  • CaptiveAire: 45 to 60 degrees [1].

At 45 degrees the anchor sits as far out from the mount as the wire's attach point hangs below the deck. Hang a clip 6 ft down the tube and each anchor goes about 6 ft out. Walk the steel with that number before you pick a mounting point. You need a joist or purlin at all four corners.

Hardware at the structure and the fan

MacroAir wants each guy wire on a 1/2 in eye lag or bolt, and it doesn't supply them. Its kit has four wire ropes in 19, 23 or 33 ft lengths, four turnbuckles, four 1/4 in quick links and eight wire rope clamps [4].

Hunter's DDI uses beam clamps at the structure [5]. MacroAir and Hunter both say not to wrap the guy wires around the building steel [4][5].

CaptiveAire's guy wire hardware is 1/8 in cable sold in 50 ft lengths, 1/8 in cable clamps, a galvanized turnbuckle and a 1/4 in thimble. Each cable gets one thimble and two clamps, and the 1/8 in clamps torque to 3 ft-lb. In a caustic space CaptiveAire calls for 1/4 in 316 stainless for both the guy wires and the safety cables [1].

Big Ass Fans skips the turnbuckle. The wire clips to the fan with a carabiner and takes up through a Gripple [2][3]. On the Powerfoil X, the second wire rope clip goes 3 to 4 in from the first, torqued to 4.5 ft-lb [2].

Tensioning

Snug is the target. Hunter and MacroAir tighten the turnbuckles by hand in a crisscross pattern, and Hunter has you check that the downrod stays plumb and the fan level as you go [4][5]. CaptiveAire says snug, and no tighter than the point where the fan starts going out of level [1].

On Big Ass Fans units, cinch all four Gripples evenly and keep 6 to 8 in between the Gripple and the eyebolt on a Powerfoil X, or between the Gripple and the carabiner on the smaller fans. Big Ass Fans warns that over-tightening can throw the fan off balance [2][3].

Safety cable on every fan

All four want a safety cable, and two of them tie it to the warranty.

  • CaptiveAire: safety cables at every connection, two clamps on every cable end. The U-bolt goes on the short turnback and the saddle on the live side [1].
  • MacroAir: safety cable on every fan, and the warranty depends on it. The kit cable is 1/8 in wire rope [4].
  • Hunter: the retention cable goes on every fan, and skipping it voids the warranty. Wrap it around structure that can hold double the installed fan weight, set the second clamp about 6 in from the first and leave about 3 in of slack [5].
  • Big Ass Fans Powerfoil X: draw the cable tight around the I-beam or angle iron with as little slack as possible, and close it with a shackle [2].

Hunter wants 3 in of slack and Big Ass Fans wants none, so on a mixed-brand job set each cable to its own fan's rule.
