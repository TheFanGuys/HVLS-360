# HVLS 360° — Editorial Copy Desk

**Internal editorial standard; not a reader-facing page.**

## What a useful story has to deliver

Every finished article needs a specific reason to exist, primary sources with dates/versions, an answer to “what changed or what can a field person do with this?”, and clear separation between measured evidence, manufacturer claims and the magazine's analysis. The goal is to earn the attention of working installers, engineers, facility operators **and** fan manufacturers.

## Two independent passes, not one AI-generated polish

**Pass 1 — Evidence and technical editor**

- Identify every specific product, date, number, electrical claim, standard edition and test outcome; give it a source, calculation or on-record field observation.
- Verify primary sources in full context; an official product page establishes the manufacturer's claim, not independent effectiveness.
- Distinguish a product manual from broader industry practice. Verify the **exact model** before copying a drive, fire-relay or mounting detail.
- Identify uncertain assumptions and remove or qualify them where material. Never pretend a test or interview happened.

**Pass 2 — Human-sounding copy editor**

- Replace promotional fog (“revolutionary solution,” “game-changing efficiency,” “unlock the power of airflow”) with tangible observations.
- Open with the most useful finding, not a boilerplate paragraph about the importance of ventilation.
- Give short, sharp section headings. Vary sentence lengths. Avoid redundant recaps and padded “key takeaways.”
- Trim generic disclaimers and process language from the published article; leave necessary context, sourced technical precautions and legal disclosures.
- End with the unresolved tradeoff, reader action, or observed result—not “the future is bright.”
- Read aloud: if any paragraph could be swapped into an unrelated HVAC story without changing meaning, rewrite it.

## Release review record

A copy editor signs off with the article's final source list, checked outbound URLs, specific edits and any open editorial questions. A different qualified reviewer handles consequential technical judgments; no script or editorial label is a substitute for professional review. The publisher has the final release decision.

## Product reviews and videos

Use **First look / manual analysis** when no sample has been operated. Reserve **Hands-on review** for real documented testing with identifiable setup, dates, measurements and limitations. Sponsored equipment and editorial relationships must be disclosed when applicable. AI-generated visuals can illustrate concepts but cannot stand in for a filmed installation or a measured test.
