# HVLS 360° — Editorial Copy Desk

**Internal editorial standard; not a reader-facing page.**

## What a useful story has to deliver

Every finished article needs a specific reason to exist, primary sources with dates/versions, an answer to “what changed or what can a field person do with this?”, and clear separation between measured evidence, manufacturer claims and the magazine's analysis. The goal is to earn the attention of working installers, engineers, facility operators **and** fan manufacturers.

## Two independent passes, not one AI-generated polish

**Pass 1 — Evidence and technical editor**

- Identify every specific product, date, number, electrical claim, standard edition and test outcome; give it a source, calculation or on-record field observation.
- Verify primary sources in full context; an official product page establishes the manufacturer's claim, not independent effectiveness.
- Distinguish a product manual from broader industry practice. Verify the **exact model** before copying a drive, fire-relay or mounting detail.
- Identify uncertain assumptions and remove or qualify them where material. Never pretend a test or interview happened.

**Pass 2 — Human-sounding copy editor**

- Replace promotional fog (“revolutionary solution,” “game-changing efficiency,” “unlock the power of airflow”) with tangible observations.
- Open with the most useful finding, not a boilerplate paragraph about the importance of ventilation.
- Give short, sharp section headings. Vary sentence lengths. Avoid redundant recaps and padded “key takeaways.”
- Trim generic disclaimers and process language from the published article; leave necessary context, sourced technical precautions and legal disclosures.
- End with the unresolved tradeoff, reader action, or observed result—not “the future is bright.”
- Read aloud: if any paragraph could be swapped into an unrelated HVAC story without changing meaning, rewrite it.

## Release review record

A copy editor signs off with the article's final source list, checked outbound URLs, specific edits and any open editorial questions. A different qualified reviewer handles consequential technical judgments; no script or editorial label is a substitute for professional review. The publisher has the final release decision.

## Product reviews and videos

Use **First look / manual analysis** when no sample has been operated. Reserve **Hands-on review** for real documented testing with identifiable setup, dates, measurements and limitations. Sponsored equipment and editorial relationships must be disclosed when applicable. AI-generated visuals can illustrate concepts but cannot stand in for a filmed installation or a measured test.


## Handoff accountability

The Editorial Director owns the voice pass before a draft enters the founder's reading queue. Record the manuscript revision, concrete edits and unresolved questions in its review record. A workflow run, word count, writing plugin or AI-detector score does not establish that this pass happened. Internal verification checklists belong in the companion review record; retain material qualifications in the article itself. Do not pad copy to meet a minimum word count.


## Standing founder requirement — all current and future articles

Chris's November 11, 2026 direction applies to the entire queue and every article produced afterward. A separate voice edit is mandatory before any founder handoff, regardless of author, department, automation or plugin used. Do not ask Chris to identify or repair generic AI prose.

The Editorial Director owns this pass; the Managing Editor verifies its record before presenting copy. Record the manuscript SHA-256, date, editor role, concrete changes and unresolved issues in the companion review. Any later manuscript change requires a new voice recheck. A note without a matching revision is not completion. Technical review, fact checking and explicit founder approval remain separate requirements.

Read for repetitive warnings, interchangeable introductions, vague headings, forced contrasts, formulaic lists, redundant recaps and process notes that belong in the review record. Preserve necessary qualifications and source attribution. Do not introduce invented experience, quotes, slang or unsupported certainty to make writing sound human. No plugin output or AI-detector score substitutes for editorial judgment.

## Founder direction — voice, design and working style

Chris reaffirmed November 11: the entire publication must read and look like a serious trade magazine. The assistant does the work; Chris makes editorial and business decisions. Do not make him clean up AI prose or supervise routine execution.

- Write directly, with concrete field detail, useful reporting and natural sentence rhythm. Cut padded intros, generic transitions, forced contrasts, repeated caveats and formulaic conclusions.
- Keep legal boilerplate, review checklists, approval reminders, confidence labels and internal process commentary out of reader-facing articles and artwork. Keep a short qualification only where it changes the reader's understanding of a claim or action. State it once, where relevant.
- Do not add warnings, badges, disclaimers or requests for outside experts reflexively. Identify the actual unsupported claim, verify it, narrow it or remove it. Request specialist input only for a specific consequential question that cannot be resolved responsibly from available evidence. Record outstanding work internally.
- Use restrained trade-publication design: strong typography, legible diagrams, accurate relevant field images and clean spacing. Avoid generic AI artwork, decorative dashboard clutter, excessive cards, icons and status banners. Review phone presentation as well as desktop.
- Apply this to all current/future stories, office screens, graphics and user updates, regardless of plugin or author. Separate factual verification from voice and visual editing. Do not invent experience or certainty.
- Keep updates short: result, real obstacle if any, next action. Maintain the publication approval control internally; do not append the same warning to every reply.
