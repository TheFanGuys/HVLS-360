# HVLS 360 — Editor-in-Chief Assignment Desk V1

This system produces **article assignment briefs**, not finished articles. Each brief records a research lead, topic, original link, unanswered questions and mandatory checks. The Research Director's conservative screening holds obvious market-report spam and unrelated headlines.

## Editorial stages
1. **Research required** — lead identified; primary source not verified.
2. **Source verified** — original document, author, publication date, technical evidence and U.S. applicability confirmed by editor.
3. **Draft in progress** — original article written with citations and practical field implications.
4. **Technical review** — verify installation, safety, electrical, regulatory and engineering statements.
5. **Visual review** — image authenticity, permission, technical accuracy, alt text and mobile presentation.
6. **Publisher review** — complete draft and supporting references presented for explicit approval.
7. **Approved for publication** — only publisher authorization permits publication.

## Critical safeguards
- The generated JSON is kept in a GitHub Actions report artifact, not the public research index.
- Headlines must not be treated as confirmed facts or publishable copy.
- No agent is allowed to fabricate field experience, research results, citations or manufacturer endorsements.
- No automated article publishing, external outreach or spending.
- Source verification and article drafting are still future implementation steps.

## Intended publisher experience
A future authenticated dashboard should present completed drafts, source links, technical review notes, image comparisons and approve/reject controls. Today's local-only dashboard does not implement that workflow.
