# HVLS 360 — Field Impact Alerts

## Additional desks
- **Scissor lifts, boom lifts, MEWPs:** JLG, Genie, Skyjack, Haulotte, Snorkel and other manufacturers; new equipment, height/reach, indoor tire/floor considerations, battery/charging, service, recalls and safety bulletins.
- **Rental industry:** United Rentals, Sunbelt Rentals, Herc Rentals, EquipmentShare and regional rental providers; availability, fleet changes, rental economics, delivery/collection, damage policies, insurance, jobsite access and market consolidation.
- **Construction pipeline:** warehouses, logistics, cold storage, manufacturing, agriculture, large industrial expansions and building renovations that could create demand for HVLS installation and service.
- **Jobsite implications:** lift selection, floor loading, ceiling heights, racking, obstructions, overhead work coordination, sprinkler/fire alarm integration, lead times and electrical scope.

## Field-impact scoring
For every lead ask:
1. Does it affect safety, compliance, installation methods or equipment choice?
2. Could it change estimates, labor time, access equipment cost or job scheduling?
3. Is there a verifiable new product, rule, construction project or market event?
4. Does it affect Arizona, Utah, California, Texas or other Big Fan Guys service areas?
5. Is it source-verified, or merely an unconfirmed news-search candidate?

Flag regulatory items by jurisdiction, adoption status and effective date. Do not confuse manufacturer claims, voluntary standards, proposed rules and binding requirements.
