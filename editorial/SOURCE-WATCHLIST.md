# HVLS 360 — Source & Regulatory Intelligence Policy

## Source coverage
Monitor manufacturer websites, newsrooms, press releases, product pages, manuals, recalls, service bulletins, and industry news. Initial watchlist includes:
- HVLS and ventilation: Big Ass Fans, Rite-Hite, Vivid Air, MacroAir, Go Fan Yourself, Patterson Fan, Hunter Industrial, Greenheck, Airius, Refresh Fans, Canarm, Jan Fan and additional relevant manufacturers as discovered.
- Drives/controls: ABB, Invertek Drives, Danfoss, Yaskawa, Schneider Electric, Siemens, Rockwell Automation, WEG, other VFD/automation suppliers.
- Smart/AI controls: Thingz (verify exact company/product identity and applicability before publishing), sensor-based fan control, BMS integrations, occupancy, temperature and air-quality control, predictive maintenance.

## Regulatory desk
Prioritize primary authority sources:
- OSHA (rules, proposed rules, guidance, interpretations, enforcement developments)
- NFPA (adopted standards and proposed revisions, notably sprinkler and electrical interactions)
- UL / ANSI / AMCA (product and performance standards)
- ICC / IBC / IMC / IFC, NEC/NFPA 70 and applicable local amendments
- State and municipal building, electrical, mechanical and fire departments; AHJs
- State occupational safety plans and heat-related requirements when relevant

For every regulatory item capture:
1. Issuing authority and jurisdiction.
2. Exact citation/standard and edition.
3. Whether **binding rule, adopted code, proposed change, guidance, interpretation or marketing claim**.
4. Adoption/effective date, applicability, exceptions, and local amendments.
5. Original authoritative URL and date checked.
6. Field implications for mounting, sprinkler interaction, fire alarm shutdown, wiring, commissioning, service or customer advice.
7. Uncertainties and required AHJ/manufacturer verification.

**Never infer universal compliance from a national model code.** Local adoption and AHJ interpretation may differ. Do not publish unsupported legal conclusions.

## Research workflow limitations
The initial RSS searches are **discovery only**, not guaranteed direct monitoring of every named official newsroom. A dedicated primary-source crawler and editorial verification stage remain to be implemented. The Tuesday/Friday schedule is for leads, not for automatically publishing articles.

## Editorial credibility
Separate press-release claims from independent testing and firsthand field experience. Do not imply that Big Fan Guys manufactures fans, represents every manufacturer, or performed a test it did not perform. Label sponsorships and affiliate relationships clearly.
