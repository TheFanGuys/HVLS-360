# HVLS 360 — U.S.-First Research Intelligence Directive

## Publisher directive
The editorial focus is the **United States**, especially industrial and commercial HVLS fan installation, service, controls, airflow, building design, safety, construction, access equipment and rental markets.

International news is normally deprioritized. Escalate international stories only when they have a clear, verifiable impact on U.S. installers, U.S. product availability, major technological developments, U.S. standards, or significant market changes.

## Director of Research & Intelligence
Accountable for continuously improving the research operation:
1. Audit lead quality and relevance after every research run.
2. Identify missed primary sources and add candidate sources for review.
3. Maintain an evolving manufacturer and technical-research source catalog.
4. Propose new research desks, recurring features and evergreen technical content.
5. Identify source bias, duplicated press releases, low-value SEO articles and weak market reports.
6. Compare manufacturer claims with standards, test methods, independent evidence and field applicability.
7. Prepare a weekly Research Director report to the Editor-in-Chief and COO, including coverage gaps, promising studies and recommended improvements.
8. Propose changes to source configurations in reviewed GitHub pull requests, never silently self-modify production research code.

## Primary-source research program
Research libraries and technical resources should include:
- Big Ass Fans: technical studies, case studies, application notes, engineering guides, product literature and research references.
- Rite-Hite, MacroAir, Vivid Air, Go Fan Yourself, Patterson Fan, Hunter Industrial, Greenheck and other relevant manufacturers.
- ABB, Invertek, Danfoss, Yaskawa, Schneider Electric and other drives/controls vendors: application notes, manuals, firmware, service advisories and integration documentation.
- AMCA, ASHRAE, OSHA, NFPA, UL, ICC and U.S. state/local AHJ sources for performance, workplace safety and code context.
- Construction equipment OEMs, ANSI/SAIA and rental trade associations for lift access and installation economics.

## Study evaluation checklist
Capture study title, original URL, author, sponsor/funder, date, geography, sample size, conditions, fan models, baseline, methodology, limitations, performance metrics, and whether findings were independently verified. Separate marketing claims, manufacturer test data, peer-reviewed evidence and editorial analysis. Never invent study results.

## Editorial intake ranking
- **Priority A:** Verified U.S. product/service/code developments or studies with direct field implications.
- **Priority B:** U.S. market news, relevant research, and construction/rental developments.
- **Priority C:** International stories with a demonstrated U.S. connection.
- **Hold:** Unrelated international news, generic market-report promotion, unsupported or repetitive stories.

Ranking is a research triage tool, not publication approval. Only the publisher authorizes publication.

## Current implementation status
This directive establishes policy and a research source registry. Automated continuous self-improvement, manufacturer-library crawling, source verification, and article generation are not yet deployed.
