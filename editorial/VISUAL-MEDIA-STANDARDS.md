# HVLS 360 — Visual Media & Art Department

## Mission
Produce authentic, technically credible, publication-quality imagery for HVLS 360. Avoid generic AI-looking art, inaccurate equipment depictions and visual filler.

## Reporting
Director of Visual Media reports to Editor-in-Chief and COO. Publisher approves all public articles and substantive editorial imagery.

## Roles
1. **Photo Editor:** crop, straighten, exposure, white balance, natural color, noise reduction and output sharpening. Retain original.
2. **Art Director:** consistent editorial design, masthead, story covers, infographics and typography.
3. **Visual Fact-Checker:** inspect fan blade count, motor/mounting geometry, wiring, PPE, clearances, site context, labels and any apparent installation claims; flag ambiguity for human review.
4. **Digital Asset Manager:** image provenance, descriptive filename, alt text, caption, copyright/permission, size, format and mobile optimization.

## Source priority
1. Original Big Fan Guys field photographs, including permitted assets from https://bigfanguys.com.
2. Manufacturer-provided press or product imagery with explicit permitted editorial use and attribution.
3. Licensed stock imagery with recorded license and context.
4. AI-generated conceptual art only when justified and clearly labeled; never pass it off as actual field documentation.

## Publication gate
- Image supports the article's subject.
- No impossible or invented installation details.
- No fabricated faults, labels, components or job-site conditions.
- Cropping/color corrections do not materially mislead.
- Rights and provenance recorded.
- Mobile crop tested; image size optimized.
- Caption, alt text and attribution accurate.
- Publisher approves publication.

## Asset record
For each image retain: source URL or original file, photographer/owner, rights status, article association, edit history, caption, alt text, original filename, exported variants and reviewer decision.

## Operational status
This file defines the department and quality gate. Automated visual review agents are not yet deployed or verified.
