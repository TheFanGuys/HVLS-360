# 5 Things We Check on Every Annual HVLS Service

An HVLS fan is a big piece of machinery hanging over your people and your product. Once a year it should get a real inspection. Here's what Big Fan Guys crews go through on a standard service call.

## The checklist

- Mount and hardware. Every bolt at the structure, the mount and the hub gets checked for torque and wear.
- Safety cables and guy wires. These are the backup restraints that hold the fan if something lets go. We check them and re-tension them if needed.
- Gearbox and motor. We listen and feel for vibration, look for oil seepage, and make sure the drive runs true.
- Airfoils and winglets. We look for cracks, loose fasteners and buildup that throws the fan out of balance.
- Controls and wiring. We check the VFD, sensors and connections so the fan starts, ramps and stops the way it should.

That lines up with what fan makers ask for. Big Ass Fans' Powerfoil 6 installation guide, for one, lists annual checks of safety cables, mounting hardware, hub components and airfoils, plus watching for oil leaks and unusual noise [1].

## Why once a year matters

Catch a loose bolt or early wear on a scheduled visit and it's a quick fix. Let a worn bearing keep running and it can take out the whole hub and motor assembly. That's a much bigger job and real downtime.

Check your fan's warranty terms too. If they call for documented maintenance, the service record from that visit is your proof.
