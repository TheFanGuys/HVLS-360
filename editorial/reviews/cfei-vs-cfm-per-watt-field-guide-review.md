## Editorial note

**Useful verified technical detail:** Under Appendix U §3.5, large-diameter ceiling fans are tested at full and 40% speeds; real power must include all relevant fan-system components and phases. This rules out treating a motor nameplate watts estimate as an equivalent published CFEI test. Standard threshold numbers are federal product requirements, not a substitute for application engineering.


The strongest reader utility is the *model-comparison shopping list*, not a dramatic efficiency percentage. Preserve that practical framing. Do not imply one metric determines comfort, fire-code compliance, site energy savings or optimum placement.
