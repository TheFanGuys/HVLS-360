## Could not confirm / limits

- MacroAir manual is hosted on a Shopify CDN; its footers print macroairfans.com, but a link from MacroAir's own site to this file was not confirmed.
- MacroAir manual states the safety cable as 1/8" (pp. 13, 24, 36) and 3/16" (p. 22, glulam drill hole). Article states only the 1/8" kit cable.
- Big Ass Fans Powerfoil X guide is a 2011 revision. A current Powerfoil X4 guide could not be retrieved, so the article names the Powerfoil X and does not extend its figures to the X4.
- Hunter's current combined Titan/ECO/XP/DDI manual could not be retrieved. Article limits Hunter figures to the DDI.
- Hunter DDI does not print the guy wire count or spacing; "four" rests on the four turnbuckles and four beam clamps in the box.
- Rite-Hite Revolution manual was found but its stabilization-cable wording wasn't verified in a second pass, so Rite-Hite was left out.
- No manufacturer-hosted manuals found for Kale, SkyBlade, Envira-North or EPIC.
