# HVLS 360° Video Desk — Powerfoil 6 Field Review (Preproduction)

**Format:** 90-second documentary-style social cut + 5–7 minute detailed field review  
**Status:** Concept only. No test footage, measured results, rights or test samples secured.  
**Editorial purpose:** Use original installer footage to test what the manual says against what a field crew encounters. Do not pass manufacturer footage or simulated installation off as independently tested.

## Review promise

“Here is what the Powerfoil 6 installation documentation asks of an installer. Here is what the actual installation and commissioning looked like on this job—and what we measured.”

## Before cameras roll

Secure lawful access to a real, identified model and obtain the property owner's filming permission. Record exact SKU/serial (blur or crop if appropriate), nameplate electrical configuration, installation guide revision and controls package. If the fan is already installed, do not invent an installation story.

## Capture plan

| Sequence | Footage | On-screen factual anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00–00:12 | Wide establishing shot of real fan in context | Brand/model, facility type and exact fan diameter |
| 00:12–00:26 | Supporting steel, beam and mount detail | Actual engineered mounting approach; compare against manufacturer guide |
| 00:26–00:43 | Onboard VFD enclosure exterior and controller operation | Controls variant; describe observed behavior only |
| 00:43–01:02 | Airflow measurement or occupied-zone observation | Instrument make/model, sensor height, location and whether fan is on/off |
| 01:02–01:17 | Service access and inspection points | Accessible annual-maintenance findings; do not imply full internal inspection without doing it |
| 01:17–01:30 | Reviewer close with a field takeaway | What worked, what would be improved, and unresolved questions |

**Long-form additions:** Installation time log, mounting coordination, electrical feeder planning, control integration, environmental measurements, safety/maintenance checklist and multiple test runs. Identify which findings are installer observations, manufacturer claims, or instrument data.

## Testing methodology

1. Document ambient conditions and location before changing fan speed. If taking airspeed readings, record sensor type, calibration status, height, map positions and repeatability.
2. Keep comfort observations separate from air-temperature measurements and separate manufacturer CFM figures from independent test results.
3. When discussing the fire-alarm interface, use manufacturer documentation and an authorized supervised demonstration coordinated with the building/fire-alarm team; no unsanctioned system test.
4. Include any real limitations: test length, ceiling height, obstructions, simultaneous HVAC operating conditions, other fans, and inability to isolate variables.
5. Record every manufacturer-supplied product, compensation, sponsorship or loaner that requires editorial disclosure. Do not accept editorial veto rights.

## Production workflow

- Research/editorial drafts script and verifies every factual screen caption.
- Technician/reviewer captures real jobsite footage, measurements and first-hand observations.
- Media editor cuts the 90-second and long-form versions, creates licensed/original graphics, captions and brand package.
- Technical reviewer checks installation/action descriptions; independent copy editor removes generic marketing and artificial narration.
- Publisher signs off on final rendered video and description before uploads.

## Publishing package (when production exists)

Deliver 16:9 master, 9:16 vertical cut, 1:1 thumbnail, captions/SRT, transcript, date/versioned measurement sheet, YouTube title/description/chapter plan, source and media-rights inventory. No fabricated footage, invented measured values, or generic AI product-video claims.
