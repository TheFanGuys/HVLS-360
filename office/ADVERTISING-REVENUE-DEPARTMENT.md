# HVLS 360 — Advertising & Revenue Department

## Mission
Build sustainable revenue from a niche HVLS and industrial air movement publication without compromising technical credibility or the publisher's budget.

## Financial policy — $0 default
- No paid ads, ad boosts, hosting, SaaS, subscriptions, API credits, purchased lists, agency fees, sponsorship fees, or other expenditures without the publisher's **specific case-by-case approval**.
- No automatic spending or recurring payment commitments. No credit card or billing details in repository files.
- Each proposed expense must include vendor, purpose, total and recurring cost, duration, expected benefit, free alternative, cancellation terms, and an explicit approval decision.
- Free options first: organic search, permitted social distribution, earned trade-media coverage, direct relationships, owned newsletter when available at no added cost, and free analytics where feasible.

## Agent roles
1. Advertising & Revenue Director — accountable for commercial strategy, reporting to COO.
2. Sponsor Prospecting — identifies relevant fan, drives, controls, lift, rental, safety and industrial suppliers; maintains a research-only prospect queue.
3. Sponsorship Packaging — prepares transparent media kit, ad inventory, rate card proposals, sponsored newsletter packages and clearly marked placements.
4. Ad Operations — handles approved placements, dates, asset specs, accessibility, labeling and reporting.
5. Affiliate & Partner Research — evaluates relevant, reputable programs and disclosures; no undisclosed commissions.
6. Revenue Analytics — measures qualified leads, actual revenue, conversion, retention and costs.
7. Budget Controller — requires explicit publisher sign-off for every expense and contract.

## Editorial firewall
- No sponsor may buy positive technical coverage, suppress criticism, or control independent recommendations.
- Clearly label sponsored content, paid placements and affiliate links; follow FTC endorsement disclosure requirements.
- Manufacturer logos, trademarks and photography require proper permission and attribution.
- No fabricated traffic statistics, sponsor commitments, endorsements, customer relationships or guaranteed results.
- No unsolicited bulk outreach or mass posting. Draft pitches require publisher approval before any contact.

## Revenue sequence
1. Establish accurate audience measurement and credible editorial output.
2. Draft media kit with truthful metrics only (unknowns explicitly marked).
3. Research relevant sponsor prospects; prioritize direct industry relationships.
4. Prepare individualized pitches and proposed ad inventory for approval.
5. Run limited approved campaigns, measure results, and report monthly.
6. Consider display ad networks only after eligibility, privacy and user-experience review.

## Current state
Department charter and approval queues established. No ads, campaigns, sponsor outreach, affiliate programs, paid services or revenue agents have been activated. No expenditure authorized.
