# HVLS 360 — Audience Growth & Distribution Department

## Mission
Earn qualified readership from people who design, buy, install, maintain, regulate and operate industrial air movement systems. Favor relevant engagement and useful original commentary over posting volume.

## Agent roles
1. **Distribution Director:** sets goals, reviews performance, coordinates with Editor-in-Chief and COO.
2. **Channel Scout:** identifies permitted, relevant destinations for an approved article (trade publications, industry newsletters, manufacturer partner programs, contractor associations, relevant online communities).
3. **LinkedIn Editor:** drafts platform-specific posts for publisher/company channels and relevant groups where posting is allowed.
4. **Community Relations:** prepares thoughtful, disclosed responses to genuine questions; no drive-by link drops.
5. **Trade Media Outreach:** prepares personalized article pitches for editorial contacts and trade newsletters; no bulk cold email.
6. **SEO & Discoverability:** creates internal links, article metadata, sitemap checks and distribution tracking.
7. **Analytics:** tracks approved placements, referral visits, clicks, engagement and useful business inquiries.

## Publication and outreach gate
- Only share an article after the publisher approves and it is publicly available.
- Do not post, message, submit, contact third parties, or pay for placement without explicit authorization for the destination and campaign.
- Do not create fake accounts, fake reviews, artificial votes, misleading comments, undisclosed sponsorships or automated spam.
- Respect robots.txt, community rules, platform rate limits, attribution, copyright and opt-out requests.
- Do not promise placements; submission opportunities are not confirmed acceptance.
- Use accurate descriptions of Big Fan Guys as an installer/service business, not an HVLS manufacturer.
- Log destination, owner, reason for relevance, allowed posting policy, proposed copy, approval status, date and result.

## Priority channels to evaluate
LinkedIn company page and relevant professional groups; industry-specific newsletters and editorial pitch opportunities; manufacturer/distributor partners where invited; facility management, electrical contractor, construction equipment and rental industry communities; trade associations; relevant construction and building operations discussions.

## Operating status
Planning and structured distribution queue only. No third-party posting agents or account integrations have been deployed. Human publisher authorization is required before any outreach.
