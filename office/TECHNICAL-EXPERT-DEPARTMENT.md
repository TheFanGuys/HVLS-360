# HVLS 360° Technical & Expert Department

**Purpose:** Serve the Research Desk, Managing Editor and publisher with technically literate analysis of fan engineering, VFDs, controls, HVAC/airflow, installation, safety and performance claims. Experts are editorial AI roles, not eight human employees and not eight continuously running autonomous model instances.

## Specialist assignments

| Role | Questions to answer | Evidence priority |
|---|---|---|
| HVLS mechanical specialist | Fan geometry, motors, mounting, OEM revisions | Exact fan model installation guide, product submittal |
| Electrical/VFD specialist | Supply, drive options, motor matching, EMC, derating | ABB/Invertek OEM manuals and nameplates |
| Controls specialist | Keypads, BAS, fire-alarm interfaces, network and shutdown | Model-specific controller manuals and approved sequences |
| Airflow/comfort specialist | CFM, CFEI, comfort, destratification, ventilation | AMCA, ASHRAE, DOE, measured conditions |
| Codes/safety specialist | OSHA, NFPA, electrical code, access equipment | Current official rule or standards edition |
| Manufacturer intelligence specialist | Product launch timing, revision changes, competing claims | Original manufacturer announcements and current manuals |
| Independent source checker | Unsupported statements, broken references, copied claims | Original primary sources, not press-release echoes |
| Field installation/service specialist | What changes for a working technician | Manuals, actual field conditions and publisher's expertise |

## Research-to-editor workflow

1. Research submits a lead with exact primary URL, date, model and claimed change.
2. Managing Editor assigns relevant specialist roles; not every story needs every expert.
3. AI experts identify what is **confirmed**, **manufacturer claimed**, **inferred**, and **unknown**; report exact source sections and revision dates where available.
4. Writer drafts with source citations, field implications and no invented firsthand testing.
5. Separate AI reviewer performs an adversarial technical/copy pass. Record fixes in the article's review file.
6. The publisher sees a clean article, relevant expert findings and any **specific** unresolved issue.
7. Human licensed/qualified professional input is sought only for claims that actually require it; general product news is not automatically held hostage to an external reviewer.
8. No article is published without publisher approval.

## Current execution

Eight article-specific AI technical review reports are recorded in `editorial/reviews/ai-expert-*.md`. These are completed editorial analysis artifacts, **not proof of an ongoing autonomous multi-agent runtime**. The scheduled research scripts discover leads and generate heuristic briefs; they do not themselves invoke language models or simulate human expert credentials.

## Publication decision labels

- **AI reviewed:** editorial technical analysis completed, with traceable source basis and limitations.
- **Publisher review:** copy is ready for founder evaluation; no automatic publication.
- **External verification needed:** a narrowly identified safety, engineering, legal or measurement claim needs real-world evidence or a qualified human.
- **Approved:** explicit founder approval of the exact article and media.

Never convert an AI review label into a claim of licensed engineering signoff.
